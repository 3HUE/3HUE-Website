FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is a fractional CISO?

A fractional CISO is a senior security leader who works with your organization part-time or for a fixed term. You get a CISO's strategy, oversight and accountability without a full-time executive hire.

How is a virtual CISO different from a full-time CISO?

A virtual CISO provides the same leadership — security strategy, risk decisions, audit readiness and board reporting — remotely and on a defined schedule. It costs less than a full-time role and can start right away.

What do fractional CISO services include in this program?

A fixed 30-day engagement with bi-weekly strategy sessions, a review of your security posture and an audit-ready remediation plan.

What is included in the Premium Intro Program?

It includes Insider Early Access plus vCISO strategic oversight, aligning execution with leadership priorities.

How long is the engagement?

The program runs for 30 days with a fixed scope and defined deliverables.

What access is required?

Scope is confirmed during intake and typically includes cloud, SaaS, and identity telemetry needed for defensible outcomes.

Who is this designed for?

Organizations that need immediate risk reduction plus executive alignment for sustained security outcomes.

What happens after the program?

You can remediate independently or extend into a longer-term vCISO and execution path.

Availability is limited to ensure senior-level engagement quality.
