Technical Depth

How we evaluate use cases

Each AI use case is assessed across four dimensions before being recommended for investment or sequencing.

Business value alignment

Does this use case improve revenue, reduce cost, reduce risk, or improve customer experience in a measurable way?

Delivery feasibility

Does the organization have the data, technology, and team capacity to execute this at acceptable quality?

Risk and governance

What are the model, data privacy, regulatory, and reputational risks — and are controls proportionate?

Strategic timing

Is this the right sequence relative to other initiatives, dependencies, and organizational readiness?

What we assess in vendor and platform reviews

Vendor and platform recommendations are grounded in four assessment areas that go beyond feature comparison.

Capability fit

Does the vendor's AI capability match the specific use case, data type, and operating context?

Risk and compliance posture

How does the vendor handle data, model provenance, audit trails, and enterprise security requirements?

Integration complexity

What does embedding this tool into your existing architecture, data flows, and team workflows actually require?

Total cost and lock-in

What are the full lifecycle costs, switching costs, and vendor dependency implications?
