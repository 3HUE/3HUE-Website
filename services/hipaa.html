What is HIPAA compliance?

Protecting health data, and proving that you do.

HIPAA compliance means meeting the federal rules that protect health information: the Privacy Rule, the Security Rule and the Breach Notification Rule. They apply to health plans, most healthcare providers and clearinghouses, and to the business associates that handle their data. An employer’s self-funded group health plan is a health plan under HIPAA, too.

Privacy RuleWho may use and disclose protected health information, and patients’ rights to access it.
Security RuleAdministrative, physical and technical safeguards for electronic PHI, starting with an accurate, thorough risk analysis.
Breach NotificationTell affected individuals within 60 days of discovery. For 500+ people, HHS and the media too.
Who’s coveredProviders, health plans (including self-funded employer plans), clearinghouses and their business associates.

Questions

What leadership asks first.

What is HIPAA compliance?

Meeting the HIPAA Privacy, Security and Breach Notification Rules: limiting how protected health information is used and disclosed, safeguarding electronic PHI, and notifying people when unsecured PHI is breached. Covered entities and their business associates must comply.

Is the new HIPAA Security Rule final?

No. HHS proposed a major update in January 2025, adding requirements such as MFA, encryption and a technology asset inventory. As of September 2026 it is still a proposal, with final action targeted for July 2027. OCR enforces the current Security Rule in the meantime.

Does HIPAA apply to employers?

An employer isn’t a covered entity just by having employee health information, but a self-funded group health plan is a health plan under HIPAA. In 2026 OCR announced its first settlements with self-funded employer plans, both after ransomware breaches and both citing the risk analysis.

What is a HIPAA risk analysis?

An accurate and thorough assessment of the potential risks and vulnerabilities to the confidentiality, integrity and availability of all the electronic PHI you hold (45 CFR 164.308(a)(1)(ii)(A)). It’s the Security Rule requirement OCR cites most, and it should be current, enterprise-wide and documented.

What are HIPAA penalties in 2026?

Civil money penalties were adjusted for inflation effective January 28, 2026. The calendar-year cap for violations of an identical provision is $2,190,294, though OCR applies lower caps to the lower penalty tiers under its 2019 enforcement discretion. Settlements usually add a two-year corrective action plan.

How fast must we report a HIPAA breach?

Notify affected individuals without unreasonable delay and no later than 60 days after discovery. For breaches affecting 500 or more people, notify HHS within the same window and, where 500+ residents of a state are affected, the media. Smaller breaches go to HHS in an annual log.

Do we need a business associate agreement?

Yes, with any vendor that creates, receives, maintains or transmits PHI on your behalf (45 CFR 164.308(b)(1)). A missing BAA is a common finding, and it’s exactly the kind of gap Client Vision puts on the POA&M with an owner and a date.
